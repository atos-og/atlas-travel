# Atlas naming system

## Public name

The product name is **Atlas**.

Use the name on its own in the WhatsApp profile, repository, conversation copy, screenshots, and portfolio materials. Do not append “Travel”, “Bot”, or “AI” to the product name.

## Descriptor

When context is needed, describe Atlas as:

> A personal travel assistant.

The descriptor explains the product without turning into a second name. Longer descriptions may say that Atlas helps people compare travel options and plan what to do after arriving.

## Writing rules

- Capitalize the name as `Atlas`.
- Keep the tone clear, calm, and practical.
- Avoid claims that Atlas searches every source, guarantees the lowest price, or completes a booking.
- Do not call Atlas an airline, travel agency, price-alert service, or autonomous agent.
- Keep the traveler-facing conversation in Portuguese while keeping repository documentation and commit messages in English.

## Visual pairing

Pair the Atlas wordmark with the approved route-shaped A symbol and the Midnight, Mint, and Sand palette documented in [the visual identity](VISUAL_IDENTITY.md). The symbol and wordmark should remain recognizable at small WhatsApp avatar sizes. Use the supplied identity board as the source of truth until an editable vector master is available.
